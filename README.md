# Flying Lyrics 🎵 - Romanize Your Lyrics ✨

> [!NOTE]
> Ever wanted to sing along to your favorite Japanese, Korean, or foreign songs on Spotify or YouTube Music, but existing lyrics tools can't even romanize the letters? **Flying Lyrics was built to solve that!** Floating synchronized lyrics, instant romanization, and translations—all in one place.

## ⚡ Quick Start (Ready in 10 Seconds!)

1. **Install the Extension** from the Chrome Web Store.
2. **Open [Spotify Web Player](https://open.spotify.com/)** or **[YouTube Music](https://music.youtube.com/)** and hit play on any track.
3. **Click the green `♫ FLYING LYRICS` button**:
   * **Spotify:** Bottom-right corner on the player bar (next to the volume and queue icons).
   * **YouTube Music:** Top-right corner of the page.

### 🪟 Always-on-Top Floating Window
* **Never switch tabs again:** Keep lyrics visible above your browser, code editor, Discord, or games.
* **Borderless Mode (Video PiP):** Want a clean look? Toggle Borderless Mode for a sleek overlay with no window borders or title bars.
* **Built-in Player Controls:** Play, pause, skip songs, adjust volume, or scrub the timeline right from the lyrics window.

### 🌍 Sing Along in Any Language
* **Triple-Layer Display:** See original lyrics, phonetic romanization, and your language's translation at the same time.
* **Instant Romanization:** Automatically converts Japanese (Romaji), Korean (Romaja), and Chinese (Pinyin) characters into easy-to-read alphabet text.
* **Automatic Translations:** Translates lyrics into 29 languages using Google Translate with seamless offline fallbacks.
* **Sync Status Badge:** Instantly see if lyrics are **Synced** (Emerald Green), **Unsynced** (Slate Gray), or an **Instrumental/No Lyrics** track (Amber).

### 🛠️ Total Control & Easy Fixes
* **Auto-Search Multi-Provider:** Automatically finds lyrics from LRCLIB, NetEase, or your own local `.lrc` files.
* **Sync Timing Tweaks:** Song a split-second ahead or behind? Tap the offset buttons (`-0.5s` / `+0.5s`) to lock it in. The extension remembers your adjustments for every song!
* **Built-in Lyrics Editor:** Fix typos, adjust timestamps, or add missing lines with the single-click lyrics editor.
* **Settings Backup:** Export and import your custom themes and timing settings across computers with a single click.

## 💡 Tips & Handy Shortcuts

* **Auto-Launch:** Want the floating window to open by itself when music starts? Turn on **"Auto-Launch Lyrics"** in the extension settings.
* **Album Art Only:** Prefer watching just the album art? Enable **"Album Cover Mode"** to hide the text and enjoy the visuals.
* **Manual Search:** Can't find lyrics for a rare song or remix? Click the search icon in the popup to search LRCLIB or NetEase manually.
