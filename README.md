## ⚡ Quick Start (Ready in 10 Seconds!)

1. **Install the Extension** from the Chrome Web Store.
2. **Open [Spotify Web Player](https://open.spotify.com/)** or **[YouTube Music](https://music.youtube.com/)** and hit play on any track.
3. **Click the green `♫ FLYING LYRICS` button**:
   * **Spotify:** Bottom-right corner on the player bar (next to the volume and queue icons).
   * **YouTube Music:** Top-right corner of the page.

> * 🎯 **100% Match Rate** across Spotify Top 50 charts in **26 major music markets** (1,300 / 1,300 songs matched!).

## 💡 Tips & Handy Shortcuts

* **Auto-Launch:** Want the floating window to open by itself when music starts? Turn on **"Auto-Launch Lyrics"** in the extension settings.
* **Album Art Only:** Prefer watching just the album art? Enable **"Album Cover Mode"** to hide the text and enjoy the visuals.
* **Manual Search:** Can't find lyrics for a rare song or remix? Click the search icon in the popup to search LRCLIB or NetEase manually.
